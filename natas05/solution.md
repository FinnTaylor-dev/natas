# Natas5 writeup

Upon entering the site you are met with the following text.
"Access disallowed. You are not logged in"

This was a clue relating to cookies.

## What are cookies

Cookies are usually a plain text file stored client side. They are used to keep you logged into sites among other things.

How to find the cookies.

Inspect the page and navigate to the storage tab.

Click on the cookies and you will see
```loggedIn = 0```
change the 0 to a 1 and you are good to go to reload the page.
```loggednIn = 1```
